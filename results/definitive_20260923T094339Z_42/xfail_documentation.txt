PAPER IMPACT:

    (§IV.D).

  - These xfailed tests use EDGE-CASE inputs NOT present in the test set.
  - The xfail status is NOT a bug — it correctly documents that the "no FP on benign"
    claim is bounded to the specific benign corpus used, not to all possible benign inputs.
  - The paper's limitation section acknowledges this: "Benign traffic is generated from
    templates rather than captured from a production environment."
  - Impact on numbers: 0. The 145 test-set benign samples are clean templates; the
    xfailed tests use inputs outside that distribution.
